Spatio-Temporal Deep Learning

🚦 Traffic Prediction & Route Optimization

A deep learning system that combines Graph Attention Networks and LSTM to model spatial relationships between connected roads and temporal traffic patterns.

Traffic Prediction View Traffic Map

What does the system do?

🗺️ Road Graph

The Electronic City / Bommasandra road network is represented as a graph where road segments form the graph nodes and connected roads form graph edges.

🕸️ GAT

Graph Attention Networks learn spatial dependencies between neighboring road segments.

⏱️ LSTM

LSTM processes historical traffic observations to learn temporal patterns in traffic conditions.

🛣️ Route Optimization

Predicted future road speeds are used to identify preferable roads and support route selection.

Model

Input

12 historical time steps × 30 road segments × 8 traffic features.

GAT

Two Graph Attention layers with 64 hidden dimensions and four attention heads.

LSTM

A 128-dimensional LSTM models the temporal evolution of each road segment.

Output

One predicted future speed for every selected road segment.
